?

input() asks

name = input("Your name? ") keeps the answer in the box name.

"7"

Answers are always text

Even if you type 7, input() gives back the text "7". To do math, change it with int().

STEP BY STEP

From answer to number.

int() turns text with digits into a real number.

age = input("How old are you? ")PyBot asks. You answer 9. The box age keeps the text "9".
age = int(age)int() turns "9" into the number 9.
next_year = age + 1Now math works: 9 + 1 = 10.
print("Next year you will be", next_year)Shows: Next year you will be 10
