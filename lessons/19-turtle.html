LOOK FIRST

Read it line by line.

Moves on the left, the pen on the right.

import turtleBrings in the turtle tools.
t = turtle.Turtle()Makes a turtle and keeps it in the box t.
t.forward(100)Walks 100 steps to the right and draws a line.
t.left(90)Turns one corner to the left. It does not walk.
t.forward(50)Now it faces up, so this line goes up.
t.color("blue")The pen draws in blue from now on.
t.pensize(5)A thicker pen: lines are 5 wide.
t.penup()Lifts the pen. The turtle walks without drawing.
t.forward(40)Moves 40 steps and leaves no line.
t.pendown()Puts the pen down. The next steps draw again.

Color names: The turtle knows English color names in quotes, like "red", "green" or "orange".
