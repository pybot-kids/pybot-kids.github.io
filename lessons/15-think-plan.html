LOOK FIRST

How to plan a program.

1

Input, work, output

What comes in? What do you do with it? What comes out?

2

Plan in # comments

Write each step in plain words after a #. Python skips those lines.

3

Pick a tool

For each step, choose a box, an if, a loop, or a function.

Pick the tool: keep something → box =. Choose → if. Repeat → loop. Use the same steps many times → function def.

LOOK FIRST

From plan to Python.

Each step of the plan becomes one line of Python.

print("Each friend gets", each)Plan: show the answer. The output.
