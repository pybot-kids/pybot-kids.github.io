LOOK FIRST

Three tools for art.

1

A function draws

def square(size, fill) keeps the steps. square(50, "red") draws one.

2

Fill it in

color("black", "yellow"): black pen, yellow inside. begin_fill() and end_fill() wrap the shape.

3

Jump with goto

penup(), goto(x, y), pendown() moves the turtle to a new spot without a line.

LOOK FIRST

Read it line by line.

A painted square on the left, a jump on the right.

def square(size, fill):A function with two parameters: how big, and which paint.
t.color("black", fill)Black pen. The inside gets the color in fill.
t.begin_fill()Starts the shape that will get painted.
for i in range(4):The square loop from the last page.
t.forward(size)Each side is as long as size.
t.left(90)One corner.
t.end_fill()Pours the paint inside the square.
def move(x, y):A helper to jump to any spot.
t.penup()Lift the pen: no line while jumping.
t.goto(x, y)Go to x (sideways) and y (up or down). The middle is (0, 0).
t.pendown()Pen down, ready to draw.
move(-50, 20)Jumps to 50 left and 20 up.
square(60, "pink")Draws a pink square there.

The screen: It is 400 steps wide and 400 tall. x goes from -200 to 200, and y too.
