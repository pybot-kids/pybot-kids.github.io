A pattern is a part that repeats.

Things come back

Shapes, colors, sounds, or dance steps that come back in the same order make a pattern.

●■

Find the core

The core is the smallest part that repeats. In circle, square, circle, square, the core is circle, square.

3×

A loop repeats the core

Draw the core 3 times and you have the whole pattern. That is what a loop does!
