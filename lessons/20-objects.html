Things that know and do.

In Python, a text or a list is an object. It keeps data (what it knows) and has methods (what it can do).

IN REAL LIFE

Things know stuff and do stuff.

Look around you. Each thing has data (what it knows) and actions (what it does).

A dog

LOOK FIRST

Data and actions, joined by a dot.

📋

Data: what it knows

A list knows the things inside it. A text knows its letters.

🏃

Methods: what it does

You already used them: word.upper() and toys.append("ball").

.

The dot

thing.job() asks the thing to do its job. The ( ) means: do it now!

STEP BY STEP

Two objects you already know.

A list and a text. Each one knows things and does things.

toys = ["car", "doll"]An object: a list. Its data is the two toys.
toys.append("ball")An action: the list adds a toy to itself.
print(toys)The data changed: 3 toys now.
word = "robot"Another object: a text. Its data is its letters.
print(word.upper())An action that gives back a new text: ROBOT
print(type(word))type() tells what kind of object it is: <class 'str'>. Remember that word class!

A secret: In the turtle zone, t = turtle.Turtle() made an object too. t knew its place and its color, and t.forward(50) was its action.
