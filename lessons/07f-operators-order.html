A tip: Two signs on the same turn go left to right. Not sure? Add parentheses. They make the order clear for Python and for people.

STEP BY STEP

Work it out step by step.

Python solves one sign at a time, in its order.

2 + 12 > 103 * 4 is 12. Now + has its turn.
14 > 102 + 12 is 14. The comparison goes last.
