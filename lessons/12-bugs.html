A TRUE STORY

The bug that was a real bug.

In 1947, a team working with the scientist Grace Hopper found a real moth stuck inside a giant computer called the Mark II. They taped it into their notebook and wrote: "First actual case of bug being found."

It was funny because engineers already called mistakes "bugs". This time, the bug was real!

BUGS IN EVERYDAY STEPS

Plans can have bugs too.

Remember algorithms? A plan is a list of steps. A bug is a step that is wrong, missing, in the wrong place, or one that never lets the plan end.

🔀Wrong orderThe steps are right, but one is in the wrong place.
❓Missing stepSomething important was never written.
✗Wrong stepA step does the wrong thing, or asks the wrong question.
♾️Never endsA step repeats and nothing tells it to stop.

Example: Get ready to play outside

  1. 1

    Put on your shoes.

  2. 2

    Put on your socks.

  3. 3

    Go outside.

🐞 Bug! Socks go on before shoes. This is a wrong-order bug.
