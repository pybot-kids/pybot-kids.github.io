LOOK FIRST

How loops make shapes.

1

Repeat the side

A square is "forward, turn 90" repeated 4 times.

2

360 ÷ sides

All the turns add up to 360. Triangle: 120. Hexagon: 60.

3

Circles too

circle(50) draws a circle. 50 is the radius: middle to edge.

LOOK FIRST

Read it line by line.

A square on the left, a flower on the right.

for i in range(4):Repeats the lines below it 4 times.
t.forward(100)Walks one side.
t.left(90)Turns one corner. 4 × 90 = 360, so the square closes.
for i in range(8):The outer loop: 8 petals.
for j in range(4):The inner loop draws one square.
t.forward(60)One side of the square.
t.left(90)One corner of the square.
t.left(45)After each square, turn a little: 8 × 45 = 360.

A shape trick: For a shape with equal sides, turn 360 ÷ the number of sides each time.
