OUR PICKS

What María learns with.

PyBot is not the only way María Ángel learns. These are robots, kits and games she loves. Maybe one is fun for you too!

  • ⚙

    BUILD AND CODE

    Elecfreaks

    Kits with sensors, motors and building pieces for the micro:bit, a tiny board you can program. First you build a gadget, then you tell it what to do with blocks or with Python.

    Visit the official site (new tab)
  • ▦

    BUILD, MOVE AND DISCOVER

    LEGO Education SPIKE and BricQ Motion

    With SPIKE you build LEGO models with motors and sensors and program them with blocks, or with Python on some sets. BricQ Motion has no electronics: you build to explore pushes, pulls and balance.

    Visit the official site (new tab)
  • ●

    A ROBOT BALL

    Sphero Mini

    A little robot ball, about the size of a ping-pong ball. You can drive it from a phone or tablet, and program how it rolls and lights up by drawing, with blocks or with text code.

    Visit the official site (new tab)
  • ◼

    A WORLD FOR LEARNING

    Minecraft Education

    The Minecraft world, made for learning. It has lessons about coding, science and more, and you can program a robot helper called the Agent with blocks or with Python.

    Visit the official site (new tab)
  • ◆

    TABLET + REAL PIECES

    Osmo

    Games that mix a tablet with real pieces on the table. In Osmo Coding you line up blocks you can touch to guide a character, step by step.

    Visit the official site (new tab)
  • ✋

    PIECES ON THE SCREEN

    PlayShifu Tacto

    Real pieces you place right on a tablet screen to play. Tacto Coding turns blocks you can hold into commands that move the story forward.

    Visit the official site (new tab)

For grown-ups

Nobody pays us to show these, and the links go straight to each official site, with no tracking. Ages, prices and versions change, so check there before you buy. You don't need any of them to learn with PyBot.
