IN REAL LIFE

Surprises in real life.

Nobody knows the result before it happens. That is random.

🎲

A dice

It can land on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6. You can't know which one.

🎩

Names from a hat

Close your eyes and pick one paper. Any name can come out.

🪙

Heads or tails

A coin flip picks one of two sides. Each turn can be different.

LOOK FIRST

Bring the toolbox, then use it.

📦

import random

Brings the random toolbox into your program. Write it once, at the top.

🎲

random.randint(1, 6)

Gives back a whole number from 1 to 6. Both ends count.

🎩

random.choice(list)

Picks one thing from a list, like a name from a hat.

STEP BY STEP

A dice in a few lines.

Each time you run it, the number can change.

import randomOpen the random toolbox.
dice = random.randint(1, 6)Roll: the box dice keeps a number from 1 to 6, like 4.
print("You rolled", dice)Shows something like: You rolled 4
if dice == 6:A question, like in Choose a path.
print("Lucky six!")Only shows when the dice gives 6.
