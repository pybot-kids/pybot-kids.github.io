Read the notes line by line.

Lines with # are for people. The others are for Python.

# Plan: count the cookies for the partyThe plan, for people. Python skips it.
cookies = 12Python runs this: a box with 12.
# Mia brings 6 more for her friendsExplains WHY the next line adds 6.
cookies = cookies + 6Now the box keeps 18.
# print("Not yet!")A line switched off with #. Python skips it.
print("Cookies:", cookies)A blank line before it groups the steps: count first, then show.
